Create your own sensitive-area KML for Hot Air

Choose your flying area and filter your own BBAC KML for use in Hot Air.

Why I made this

My usual flying area is in the Cotswolds, so I had to load three sensitive-area (SA) zones into Hot Air. I didn’t mind doing this, but recently the app became so slow that it was almost unusable. The combined area was large and contained so many SAs.

First, I upgraded my iPad. That helped a little, but it still wasn’t fast enough. So I decided to make this planner — with AI doing the coding — to create a smaller KML covering just the area where I might fly. It’s simple, and it has made Hot Air run nice and fast on my iPad again. If I’m flying in another part of the country, I can just generate a new local file.

I made this planner so I could select the latest national BBAC KML, choose where I’m flying, and generate a smaller file for that area. I thought other pilots might find it useful too. It’s free, and I’d welcome suggestions.

I’m also tempted to explore combining sensitive areas and UK airspace in one file to see whether that could make Hot Air faster still. That’s an idea for the future; this planner currently filters sensitive-area KML only.

— Alan Turner, Turner Balloons Limited

Help / How to use — including where to get the KML

Visit the BBAC Members Area, open Digital Mapping, and download the latest whole-country sensitive-area KML file. Save it to your device, then select it below. Use the original file supplied to you as an authorised member and check its publication date.

The national database is not provided or hosted here. If BBAC publishes an updated version, download the new full-country file and generate a new local KML. Your saved flying-area settings may be reused.

  1. Download the latest whole-country KML from BBAC Members Area → Digital Mapping, then choose that file below.
  2. Enter the postcode where you’re flying, or use the advanced coordinate fields.
  3. Choose a circle or independent North, East, South and West distances. Saving an area is optional.
  4. Optionally press Preview area on map to check the selection. Then press Generate KML and Download filtered KML.
  5. Import the smaller file into Hot Air or your own mapping app. Check the retained areas against the original KML and current official restrictions before flying.

On an iPad, look in Safari’s Downloads list or Files → Downloads.
